# Rossi Installations Ltd — Employee Handbook (extract)

## Section 6 — Annual leave and absence

### 6.1 Entitlement
All full-time employees are entitled to **25 days of paid annual leave per year, plus the 8 English bank holidays**. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement. The leave year runs from **1 April to 31 March**.

### 6.2 Requesting leave
Leave must be requested through the online rota system with **at least 2 weeks' notice**. Requests for more than 5 consecutive working days require 4 weeks' notice. All requests are approved by the Branch Manager, **Sarah Whitfield**, taking into account workload and the number of engineers already on leave: no more than 2 engineers may be on leave at the same time during the heating season (October to March).

### 6.3 Carry-over
A maximum of **5 days** of unused leave may be carried over into the next leave year and must be taken by 30 June. Any further unused days are lost, except where leave could not be taken because of sickness or statutory leave.

### 6.4 Bank holidays
Engineers rostered to work on a bank holiday (emergency cover for maintenance-contract customers only) receive a day off in lieu plus the 50% uplift on hourly pay set out in Section 4.

### 6.5 Sickness absence
Employees must telephone the Branch Manager before 8:00 on the first day of absence. A self-certification form is required for absences of up to 7 calendar days; a fit note from a GP or hospital is required from the 8th day. Statutory Sick Pay applies from the 4th consecutive day of absence. The company does not operate an enhanced sick pay scheme at present.

### 6.6 Other leave
- Compassionate leave: up to 3 paid days on the death of a close family member, at the manager's discretion.
- Medical appointments: where possible, to be arranged outside working hours; otherwise, time will be made up or taken as leave.
- Parental, maternity, paternity and adoption leave: statutory provisions apply; speak to the Branch Manager as early as possible.

### 6.7 Contact
