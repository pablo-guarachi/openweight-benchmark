## Scope of works
Supply and installation of one **RX-16 AIR air-source heat pump (16 kW)** to replace the existing gas combination boiler serving the practice (ground floor and first floor, 5 treatment rooms, reception and staff area).

Included:
1. Removal and disposal of the existing gas boiler and capping of the gas supply by a Gas Safe registered engineer.
2. Supply and installation of the RX-16 AIR outdoor unit on anti-vibration mounts at the rear of the building.
3. Supply and installation of a 250-litre unvented hot water cylinder in the existing plant cupboard.
4. Replacement of 4 radiators undersized for low-temperature operation (sizes as per survey of 19 August 2026).
5. New wiring from the consumer unit, isolator and weatherproof outdoor connections.
6. Commissioning, MCS certificate, handover and user training (approx. 1 hour).

Not included: any making good of decoration, scaffolding (not expected to be required), upgrades to the electricity supply if requested by the DNO.
