# INCIDENT REPORT — Form MOD-09 (UK version)

**Rossi Installations Ltd**
**Report no.:** UK-2026-02
**Completed by:** Sarah Whitfield, Branch Manager
**Date of report:** 15 July 2026

## 1. Details of the incident
- **Date and time:** 14 July 2026, approximately 11:20
- **Location:** Kingsway Dental Practice, 214 Example Road, Leicester (rear plant area, ground floor)
- **Person involved:** Tom Okafor, Lead Engineer
- **Witnesses:** Daniel Price (engineer)
- **Type of incident:** slip from a stepladder

## 2. Description
Tom Okafor was standing on the third rung of a stepladder fixing a wall bracket for a pipe run when his right foot slipped and he fell approximately 80 cm to the floor, landing on his left knee. He reported pain and a bruise on the left knee. First aid (cold compress) was applied on site by Daniel Price. Tom continued working after a 30-minute break and completed the shift.

## 3. Injury
Bruising (contusion) to the left knee. No medical treatment required beyond first aid. **No days of absence.**

## 4. RIDDOR
**Not reportable** — the injury did not result in incapacity for more than 7 consecutive days and is not a specified injury.

## 5. Causes
- **Immediate cause:** the floor of the plant area was wet from condensation dripping from an uninsulated pipe, and this had not been noticed or marked.
- **Contributing cause:** the stepladder in use was missing one of its rubber non-slip feet.
- **Underlying cause:** the pre-work checklist did not include a check of the floor condition.

## 6. Corrective actions

| Action | Owner | Deadline | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Replace all stepladders in the Leicester branch with models fitted with non-slip feet | Sarah Whitfield | 31 July 2026 | Completed 28 July 2026 |
| Add "floor condition (dry, stable, clear)" to the pre-work checklist | Tom Okafor | 31 July 2026 | Completed |
| Toolbox talk on ladder safety for all engineers | Sarah Whitfield | 31 July 2026 | Completed 22 July 2026 |
| Share the lesson learned with Macerata head office for inclusion in procedure PR-07 | Sarah Whitfield | 31 July 2026 | Sent 16 July 2026 |
